# Neraya

## Agricultural Intelligence for the Farmer

**Know your field. Feel its pulse. Decide with confidence.**

Neraya is an agricultural intelligence platform built around a simple idea: farming is not only about what is happening in a field. It is also about the person standing in that field, trying to understand what they are seeing and decide what to do next.

A crop image can show a symptom.

Weather can provide context.

Field history can reveal a pattern.

The farmer can notice things that are difficult to capture as data.

And sometimes, what a farmer needs most is to know that they are not the only person facing a particular problem.

Neraya brings these pieces together.

It combines field information, crop observations, weather, AI-assisted analysis, field history, decision support, and a farmer-centered community experience into one platform.

The goal is not to make decisions for farmers.

The goal is to help them understand their situation better, make informed decisions, learn from real experiences, and feel less alone while doing it.

---

## The idea behind Neraya

Agricultural problems rarely happen in isolation.

A farmer may notice unusual spots on a leaf after several days of rain. Another part of the same field may look completely normal. The affected plants may be in an area where water collects. The crop may also be at a particular growth stage.

A single photograph cannot tell the whole story.

Neraya therefore looks beyond the image.

It brings together:

- What the farmer sees
- What the farmer reports
- What the field contains
- What crop is being grown
- The crop's growth stage
- Current weather
- Forecast conditions
- Previous field assessments
- Patterns that may emerge over time
- Experiences shared by other farmers

This creates a more complete picture of the situation.

---

## Our core philosophy

Neraya follows four simple stages:

**Evidence → Context → Reasoning → Action**

### Evidence

Start with what is actually known.

This can include:

- Crop images
- Reported symptoms
- Farmer observations
- Field information
- Crop information
- Previous assessments
- Weather information

### Context

Put that evidence into the situation where it happened.

Neraya considers things such as:

- Crop type
- Variety
- Growth stage
- Field location
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Rainfall
- Forecast conditions
- Previous field history

### Reasoning

Use the available evidence and context to understand what may be happening.

AI-assisted analysis helps interpret crop images and the information provided by the farmer. It also communicates missing evidence and uncertainty instead of presenting every result as a guaranteed diagnosis.

### Action

Turn understanding into something useful.

Neraya can suggest what the farmer may want to check next, what environmental factors may matter, and when additional expert guidance may be appropriate.

The final decision still belongs to the farmer.

> **Neraya doesn't predict for the farmer. Neraya helps the farmer decide.**

# More than a crop scanner

Neraya has two connected sides.

### The Field

This is where Neraya helps answer:

> **What is happening in my crop?**

The platform can bring together images, observations, weather, field information, previous assessments, and AI-assisted analysis to help the farmer understand a situation.

### The Farmer

This is where Neraya asks a different question:

> **How can I help the farmer understand, decide, learn, and feel supported?**

Farming can involve uncertainty, financial pressure, unpredictable weather, crop losses, and problems that do not always have an immediate answer.

Technology can provide information, but information alone is not always enough.

Neraya therefore also creates space for shared agricultural experiences and community connection.

The intention is simple:

**A farmer should not always have to feel like they are solving a difficult problem alone.**

---

# Community

The community layer is an important part of Neraya.

Farmers have knowledge that does not always exist in datasets.

Someone may have already faced a similar crop problem.

Someone may know how a particular symptom developed after heavy rainfall.

Someone may have learned something from a previous season that could help another farmer recognize a situation earlier.

Neraya's community experience is designed around sharing these kinds of real experiences.

It is not meant to replace agricultural experts or turn uncertain advice into fact.

Instead, it can help farmers:

- Share experiences
- Ask questions
- Learn from other farmers
- Discover similar situations
- Exchange practical knowledge
- Feel connected to people facing similar challenges

Community information should always remain distinguishable from verified expert information and AI-assisted analysis.

Most importantly, Neraya should never fabricate community activity just to make the platform look busy.

**Real experiences should create real intelligence.**

Instead of presenting the field as only a collection of numbers and tables, Neraya uses a three-dimensional crop visualization to create a more intuitive representation of the field.

The visualization can respond to:

- Selected crop
- Crop growth stage
- Assessment severity
- Weather conditions
- Time and environmental context

Neraya currently supports different crop visualization archetypes for crops such as:

Field Pulse is not intended to replace the underlying assessment.

# AI-assisted crop assessment

A farmer can select a field, upload a crop image, describe what they have noticed, and provide additional observations.

Neraya then brings together the available evidence and context before generating an AI-assisted assessment.

The assessment can include:

- Possible issue or condition
- Severity
- Assessment confidence
- Observed indicators
- Evidence used
- Missing evidence
- Contextual factors
- What to check next
- Practical decision support
- Environmental considerations
- Escalation guidance when additional expertise may be needed

For example, if a farmer reports brown lesions on rice leaves after wet weather, the system can consider the image alongside the reported symptoms, crop growth stage, field observations, humidity, rainfall, and forecast conditions.

The result is presented as an assessment based on available evidence, not as an unquestionable diagnosis.

---

# Weather intelligence

Weather is part of the field context in Neraya.

The platform retrieves real weather information for the selected field location and can use current conditions and forecast information when interpreting crop observations.

Weather information includes factors such as:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Wind
- Current conditions
- Forecast conditions

This matters because environmental conditions can influence how agricultural problems develop.

For example, persistent rainfall, high humidity, and prolonged wetness may provide important context when a farmer reports symptoms appearing after wet weather.

Neraya uses weather as context rather than pretending that weather alone can explain a crop problem.

---

# Field management

Farmers can create and manage multiple fields.

Each field can contain information such as:

- Field name
- Crop
- Variety
- Location
- Area
- Planting date
- Growth stage
- Field notes

Each field maintains its own assessment history.

This is important because information from one field should not accidentally be presented as information about another.

---

# Field history

Every assessment is associated with its field.

Farmers can return to previous assessments and understand how the condition of a field has changed over time.

The health timeline is based on actual recorded assessments.

Neraya does not invent historical scores, dates, or trends to make an empty timeline look complete.

When a field has only one assessment, the interface clearly presents it as the first recorded assessment.

As more real assessments are recorded, the timeline can show the resulting pattern over time.

---

# Neraya Assistant

Neraya also provides a conversational assistant for exploring agricultural questions and field information.

The assistant can work with relevant context from the selected field, including:

- Crop
- Variety
- Growth stage
- Field information
- Current weather
- Forecast conditions
- Previous assessments

This allows conversations to be grounded in the farmer's actual situation rather than treating every question as a completely separate generic conversation.

The assistant is designed to support understanding and decision-making.

It is not intended to replace qualified agricultural professionals.

---

# A farmer-centered experience

Neraya is built with the farmer in mind, not just the dataset.

A farmer may not describe a problem using technical agricultural terminology.

They may simply say:

> "Some leaves started turning yellow after the rain."

That observation still matters.

Neraya allows the farmer to describe what they see in their own words and combines that information with structured field data and environmental context.

The goal is to make the technology work around the farmer's experience rather than forcing the farmer to think like a machine.

---

# Responsible AI

Agricultural decisions can have real consequences.

For that reason, Neraya is designed around transparency and uncertainty.

The system distinguishes between:

- Evidence available to the system
- AI-assisted interpretation
- Missing evidence
- Environmental context
- Practical next steps
- Situations where expert evaluation may be appropriate

Neraya should not present an uncertain AI result as a guaranteed diagnosis.

It should not pretend to know something that the available evidence does not support.

When important evidence is missing, the system should say so.

This is one of the most important principles behind Neraya.

> **Better agricultural intelligence should come from better evidence, not greater confidence in an uncertain answer.**

---

# Data integrity

Neraya is intentionally designed not to manufacture information.

The platform avoids inventing:

- Field history
- Assessment dates
- Health scores
- Weather observations
- Farmer reports
- Community statistics
- Trends
- Confidence values

If information does not exist, Neraya should communicate that it does not exist.

This matters because a beautiful dashboard is not useful if the information inside it cannot be trusted.

# Security and environment variables

Neraya uses environment variables and server-side secrets for external services.

Sensitive credentials should never be committed to GitHub.

## Examples include:

VITE_SUPABASE_URL
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY
GEMINI_API_KEY
OPENWEATHER_API_KEY
SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY

The local .env file is excluded from version control through .gitignore.

The Gemini and OpenWeather API keys are handled through Supabase Edge Function secrets rather than being placed directly in frontend source code.

The Supabase service role key must remain server-side and must never be exposed in the client application.

For local development, use your own environment values and keep them out of version control.

# Running Neraya locally

You will need:

- Node.js
- npm
- A Supabase project
- The required API credentials
- Install dependencies
- npm install
- Configure local environment

Create a local .env file with the required frontend configuration:

- VITE_SUPABASE_URL=your_supabase_url
- VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=your_supabase_anon_key

Server-side API keys such as Gemini and OpenWeather should remain in Supabase Edge Function secrets.

## Start the development server
"npm run dev"

## Create a production build
"npm run build"

# Example workflow

A typical Neraya workflow can look like this:

1. Create a field

The farmer adds their field information, crop, variety, location, area, planting date, and growth stage.

2. Notice something unusual

The farmer observes a change in the crop and describes it in their own words.

3. Upload an image

The farmer uploads a photograph of the affected crop or leaf.

4. Add observations

The farmer can explain where the problem appears, how it is spreading, and anything else they have noticed.

5. Neraya adds context

The platform considers the field information, crop information, weather, forecast, and previous assessments.

6. AI-assisted assessment

Gemini analyzes the available image and context and produces a structured assessment.

7. Understand the result

The farmer can see what was observed, what evidence was used, what may be missing, and what to check next.

8. Continue the journey

The assessment becomes part of the field's history, allowing future observations to build on previous ones.

9. Connect with others

Through the community experience, farmers can eventually share real experiences and learn from people who may have faced similar situations.

## The Bigger Vision

Neraya's long-term vision is to become a place where agricultural intelligence and human experience come together. A farmer should be able to understand what is happening in their field, see how their crop is changing over time, learn from real experiences shared by other farmers, ask questions without feeling lost, and find the right kind of support when they need it. AI can help interpret evidence, weather can add context, field history can reveal patterns, and the community can bring something no model can fully provide: the experience of people who have actually lived through similar situations. Neraya aims to build that connection so that technology does not make farming feel more distant or complicated, but instead helps farmers feel more informed, more connected, and a little less alone when facing the uncertainties of agriculture.
